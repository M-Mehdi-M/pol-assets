Pol Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026
Developer: Zagros Byte · Author: MAHMOUDI Mohammadmehdi

This policy explains how the Android app Pol (package com.zagrosbyte.pol), made by Zagros Byte, handles information. Pol teaches languages.

The short version

  • Pol has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Your study progress stays on your device, in an encrypted SQLCipher database. It is never sent to us, and we run no server that could receive it.
  • We include no analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • Pol shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage data for ads, as explained below.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, and wherever else Google's consent rules apply, you are asked for consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in Settings at any time.
  • Pol downloads pronunciation audio from GitHub when you ask for it.
  • We do not sell your data. Uninstalling Pol deletes all the data Pol keeps on your device.

Information that stays on your device

Pol stores these on your phone or tablet only:

  • Study progress: completed lessons, quiz scores, flashcard review boxes, your streak, XP, achievements and the words you have learned or saved.
  • Settings: the app language, your course, daily goal, reminder time and sound settings.
  • Downloaded pronunciation audio packs, and technical data the app needs, such as the installed course version.

Study progress is kept in a SQLCipher database that is encrypted on the device, with a key held by your device's Android Keystore. It is not included in device backups, and Pol does not send it to us or to anyone else, so your progress does not move to a new phone.

You can delete your progress at any time with Progress, then Reset progress. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything in this section.

Pronunciation audio

Recordings for each target language are downloaded on demand, as one audio pack per language, from GitHub (github.com and its download servers, run by GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic request information when a pack is downloaded. Pol sends nothing else: no identifier, no account and no information about your progress. GitHub's privacy statement.

If a recording is missing, Pol may ask the text-to-speech engine installed on your device to read the word aloud. Only the word or sentence being studied is passed to that engine, never information about you. The engine is a separate app from its own provider, under that provider's terms.

Ads and the information Google collects

Pol is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and the UK). A banner appears on the learning path and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after a lesson.

To serve, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in Pol may collect the following and share it with Google:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device. Pol does not ask for location permission.
  • Interactions with the app and the ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads SDK.

Google uses this information for advertising (including personalized ads, where you allow them), for measurement, and for fraud prevention and security, under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

We ask Google to treat Pol as not directed to children and to show ads suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

All connections Pol makes are encrypted (HTTPS); the app does not allow unencrypted network traffic.

Consent and your choices (User Messaging Platform)

In the EEA and the UK, and wherever else Google's consent rules apply, Pol shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) the first time you open it, and requests no ads until that message allows it. Where a choice is required for your region, you can change it at any time in Settings, then Privacy options.

If you do not consent, Pol keeps working; you may see fewer ads, non-personalized ads, or none.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings (usually Settings, then Privacy, then Ads; the exact path depends on your device).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not receive it in the first place.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Pol uses the internet only for ads, consent and audio downloads, and notifications only for the study reminders you turn on.
  • We do not create accounts or user profiles, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

Pol is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Pol, contact us and we will help, for example by explaining how to delete the app's data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your device stays until you reset your progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall Pol.
  • We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete or export.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies. You can manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because Pol keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly in the app and in Android settings. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pol handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.
